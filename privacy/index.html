Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

EasyHomeControl does not collect any personal information.

The app talks to your thermostat through Apple’s HomeKit. At home, that happens between your own devices. When you’re away, HomeKit may pass it through your home hub and Apple, end-to-end encrypted. Nothing is sent to me, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

The app stores a small amount of information on your device so it remembers your selected home, thermostat, and temperature unit between launches. That information never leaves the device.

Tips

Tips are optional. When you open Settings, the app asks RevenueCat, the service that handles tips, for the tip prices. If you leave a tip, Apple processes the payment and RevenueCat keeps a record of the purchase under an anonymous ID, so tips can be confirmed and totaled. Neither includes your name or email address.

RevenueCat must protect that record at least as well as this policy does. The record is kept only as long as it’s needed for accounting. To have it deleted, email support@jonwhofixesthings.com with the order ID from Apple’s receipt, and I’ll have RevenueCat remove it.

Questions

If you have any questions, please email support@jonwhofixesthings.com.
